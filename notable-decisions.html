Notable Decisions

Jason Berke was the attorney on each of these matters. These case notes summarize the public court opinions and decisions from those cases. They are general information, not legal advice. Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.

Lee v Chin (App Div, 1st Dept 2026)

2026 NY Slip Op 01851, decided March 26, 2026. Jason Berke was counsel for the appellant (defendant). The First Department reversed the denial of a motion to dismiss and dismissed claims for fraudulent inducement and for a declaratory judgment about a lease end date, pointing to reviewed draft riders and the lease’s merger clause.

Read the case note →

1211 Gilbert Ave. Holdings LLC v Berri (Sup Ct, Bronx County 2025)

2025 NY Slip Op 50142(U) [85 Misc 3d 1212(A)], decided February 6, 2025. Jason Berke was counsel for the defendant (seller). The court dismissed the buyer’s complaint seeking specific performance and contract damages after a missed time-of-the-essence closing, allowed the seller to retain a $35,000 deposit and the interest accumulated on it in escrow, and cancelled a notice of pendency. The slip opinion will not be published in the printed Official Reports.

Read the case note →

Deen v Holder (Sup Ct, Queens County 2021)

Index No. 701105/2020. Inquest before Justice Timothy J. Dufficy on October 21, 2021; judgment entered November 29, 2021 (NYSCEF Document 32). Jason R. Berke was plaintiffs’ counsel and examined both plaintiffs. After inquest, the court awarded $700,000 for assault and emotional distress and $100,000 for loss of consortium, a damages award of $800,000 before interest, with interest from November 17, 2013.

Read the case note →

Ajie Chen v Deliso (App Div, 2d Dept 2019)

2019 NY Slip Op 01054 [169 AD3d 761], decided February 13, 2019. Jason Berke was of counsel for the appellant (the opinion lists Berke & Berke). The Second Department reversed the denial of a CPLR 3211(a)(7) motion and dismissed the complaint, including claims for intentional and negligent infliction of emotional distress.

Read the case note →

Gardner v Lefkowitz (S.D.N.Y. 2019)

No. 1:05-cv-05193 (DAB) (JCF), order signed January 23, 2019. The Southern District’s notice of electronic filing lists Jason Ross Berke among the attorneys of record who received the order. The court granted the motion to vacate and vacated an October 2005 default judgment of $180,493.82 in light of a Second Circuit remand. A later so-ordered stipulation dismissed the action with prejudice and stated that the claims were settled for $25,400.

Read the case note →

Courchevel 1850 LLC v Stern (E.D.N.Y. 2018)

No. 17-CV-1794 (NGG) (JO), memorandum and order filed June 28, 2018. Jason R. Berke of Berke & Berke was counsel for defendant Isaac Stern, according to the public docket. The court granted Stern’s motion to dismiss and dismissed the foreclosure complaint without prejudice, holding that RPAPL § 1304 pre-foreclosure notice must be sent to the borrower of what is at origination a “home loan,” even if the borrower later moves out of the property.
