Privacy notice

Law Offices of Jason Berke PC, Kew Gardens, Queens · Updated November 12, 2026

This notice explains what information this website collects and which outside services handle it. It covers www.queenseviction.com only.

Consultation request forms

When you submit a consultation request, the information you enter (such as your name, email address, phone number, the service you select, and your message) is sent through Formspree, a form-processing service, and delivered to the firm by email. The form also records which page of this site you first arrived on, so the firm can see which pages are helpful. Submitting a form does not create an attorney–client relationship. Please do not send confidential information until an engagement is confirmed.

Live chat

The chat window is provided by tawk.to, an outside chat service. Anything you type into the chat, including your name or phone number if you share them, is processed by tawk.to so the firm can read and answer it. Do not share confidential details in the chat.

Website measurement

The site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count page views and when a visitor taps a phone number or starts a chat, along with the page path where that happened and, for phone and chat events, the page where the visit began. It does not record URL query strings or fragments, or what you type into the form or chat.

Other services that load with the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like most websites, those services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when a page loads.
