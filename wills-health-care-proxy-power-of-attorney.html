Wills, health care proxies, and powers of attorney.

The Law Offices of Jason Berke PC assists clients with preparing and reviewing last wills and testaments, New York health care proxies, and powers of attorney. These documents address who should receive your property, who may make health care decisions if you cannot, and who may handle financial or legal matters on your behalf.

Wills

A will states how you want your property distributed and whom you want to handle your estate. We help clients prepare or review last wills and testaments.

Health care proxies

A health care proxy lets you name someone to make medical decisions if you cannot do so. We help clients prepare or review New York health care proxies.

Powers of attorney

A power of attorney lets you name someone to handle financial or legal matters on your behalf. We help clients prepare or review powers of attorney.
